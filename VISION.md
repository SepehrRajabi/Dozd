# DOZD

## Roadmap:

i have always had this idea in my head for a game that goes as follows:
you are pirate in space. there has been a very big and significent incident and now there are numerous space stations that are crawling with enemies (either alien or enemy). these space stations are massive and contain a lot of loot. Now to loot them you have to team up with other pirates to do the looting. but you also have the choice to either betray your team and try to take all the loot or be loyal and split them. i am still trying to think of ways to reward/punish based on the mission parameters and loot.

- progression / story / advancements?
- game design choices?
